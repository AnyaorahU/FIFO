# Reference research: synergieaustralia.com.au

Researched 22 Nov 2026 from the live site (10 pages) plus public sources. Used to define what
we are building. We copy the business concept and structure, not the brand, logo, photos or copy.

## What the business is

- **Synergie Australia** is the Australian arm of **Synergie SE**, a French staffing group
  (founded 1969, listed in Paris since 1984, ~800 offices in 17 countries).
- Started in Australia in 2012 (as Synaco), acquired Entire Recruitment (2019) and
  IPA Personnel Services (2024, which roughly doubled its size), rebranded as Synergie Australia in 2025.
- HQ in Adelaide; **16 branches** across VIC (4), NSW (3), QLD (5), WA (2), SA (1), NT (1).
- Positioning: a **blue-collar/industrial labour-hire and recruitment agency**, plus HR outsourcing.
  Tagline "Great businesses need great people"; closing line "Work, together".

## Two audiences (the site is split in two)

### Candidates (job seekers)
Mostly temp/labour-hire workers: drivers (C to MC licence), forklift (LF/LO), pick-packers,
warehouse, general hands, trades, machine operators, plus admin/business-support and some
senior specialists.

Journey described on the site:
1. Find a job on the job board (keyword search, saved jobs, job alerts by name + email + state)
   or apply via SEEK, or submit a general CV.
2. Upload **CV, proof of work rights, licences/tickets**; references are requested.
3. A consultant calls to learn about skills, availability and goals.
4. Screening: right-to-work check, licence validation, references, functional assessment.
5. Placement, with a site-specific induction and safety assessment before starting.
6. Timesheets and pay are handled in an external candidate portal ("Timesheets" link) plus a
   mobile app. The site mentions "prompt pay".

Content for candidates: "Your first job in temporary employment", "Are you a specialist?",
"Safety in the workplace", career advice (CV writing, cover letter, interviews).

### Companies (employers)
Services:
- **Hire talent**: Temporary recruitment and labour hire ("a day, a season, or an extended project"),
  Permanent recruitment, Specialist permanent recruitment (senior and hard-to-fill roles, separate sub-brand),
  International recruitment, Onsite solutions.
- **Outsourcing**: 360º HR solutions (HR admin, contracts, onboarding, policy, compliance), Payroll,
  Onsite & RPO, Safety consultancy (audits, on-site coaching), DEI consultancy,
  free "Workforce Health Check".
- Every service starts from a **Business Enquiry Form**: contact details, organisation, location,
  industry sector, permanent or temporary need, how they heard about the agency, message.

### International recruitment (why passports matter)
- Sources skilled workers from the group's network (UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Portugal,
  Poland, Switzerland, Belgium, Netherlands, Canada and others) for skill-shortage roles: trades,
  manufacturing, logistics, engineering.
- Employer-sponsored pathway: **subclass 482 (Skills in Demand)**. Candidates already in
  Australia on 485 / 417 / 462 / student visas can be placed without sponsorship.
- They state they are **not a registered migration agent** and give no visa advice.
- "Soft-landing" relocation support.

Manufacturing & Engineering · Transport & Logistics · Mining & Resources · Construction ·
Business Support · Facilities Services · Public Sector · Waste Management · Sustainability & Environment

## Values and tone
Collaboration ("You'll never walk alone") · Integrity · Flexibility/Agility · Authenticity · Ambition.
Tone is warm, team- and sport-flavoured, safety-focused ("Right fit, every time"). CSR is framed around
UN SDGs 3, 8, 10 and 13: safety and mental health, fair work and upskilling, inclusion of Indigenous,
disabled and migrant workers, carbon-neutral offices.

## Homepage structure
Header with a Candidates / Companies switch and mega-menus (Find a Job, Work with us, Employment advice,
Timesheets | Your challenges, Our services) → hero slider with promo cards + search →
opportunities → industries carousel → "Our teams by your side" branch finder by state →
employment news carousel → advice articles → testimonials (1/4 counter) → numbered values slider →
mobile app promo → international flags strip → footer (contact, the group, news, join us, socials,
job-alerts sign-up, Acknowledgement of Country, privacy/terms).

## Gaps between our build and the concept
| Concept on reference site | Our build today |
|---|---|
| Job board: search, job cards, saved jobs, job alerts | None (only industry cards) |
| Apply to a specific job | Only a general application |
| Right-to-work / visa status, licences (driver class, LF/LO), references | Passport + generic certificates |
| Candidates vs Companies split with mega-menus | Simple nav + one employers page |
| Business Enquiry Form for employers | Generic contact form |
| Branch finder by state | Static stat |
| International recruitment page and candidate path | Not covered |
| Advice/news articles | None |
| Admin: manage jobs, applicants per job, enquiries | Candidates + messages only |
